Shop rules for every session

  1. Check in at the front kiosk with the name you booked under.
  2. Closed-toe shoes, hair tied back, no loose sleeves near moving parts.
  3. Safety glasses on in the wood, metal and laser areas, always.
  4. Use only machines you have completed the intro training for.
  5. Clean your station and return tools 10 minutes before your slot ends.
